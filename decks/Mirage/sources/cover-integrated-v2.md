## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: an integrated background visual for a premium WHITE research presentation cover, landscape 16:9. Create an original monochrome optical sculpture on a perfectly clean PURE WHITE (#FFFFFF) seamless background. The sculpture is made of many very fine, closely spaced dark graphite and translucent clear lamellae / ribbons, bent into a tall open elliptical loop with gentle torsion. Beautiful sharp black contour edges, translucent layered refractions, an intricate but restrained moire. It must feel like precision optical engineering photographed for an elegant design publication, not a shiny sci-fi gadget. Object concentrated in the rightmost 42% of the image; the loop is oversized, cropped deliberately by the RIGHT and BOTTOM canvas edges, entering from the upper-right, so it feels like the graphic continues beyond the page rather than a separate object in a photo frame. Leave the entire left 55% COMPLETELY WHITE and empty for live black typography; no shape or shadow may enter this left area. Crisp graphite-black thin edges, moderate contrast, overall airy and mostly white. Absolutely no black background, no grey background, no horizon, no floor plane, no rectangular panels, no border, no frame, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no grid, no spheres, no blocks, no colors, no heavy cast shadow, no particles, no grain. White in the image must visually merge into a white slide canvas. Keep the focal object rich and carefully lit, but preserve abundant pure white negative space. Wide 16:9 high resolution. Original artwork; not a copy of another artist's specific work.
